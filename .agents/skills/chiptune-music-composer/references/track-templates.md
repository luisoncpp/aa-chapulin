## 1. Courtroom Cross-Examination (Moderato) — 128 Steps (8 Bars)

**Key**: D Minor (`D`, `F`, `G`, `A`, `Bb`, `C`)  
**Tempo**: 120 BPM  
**Vibe**: Calculated, analytical, rhythmic tension.

```
Bar 1-2 (Steps 0-31):   Dm - Bb (Intro theme statement)
Bar 3-4 (Steps 32-63):  Gm - A7 (Subdominant to dominant preparation)
Bar 5-6 (Steps 64-95):  Dm - F - C - Dm (Harmonic modulation & melodic climb)
Bar 7-8 (Steps 96-127): Bb - C - Asus4 - A7 (Climax turnaround into snare fill)
```

### Channel Blueprints:
- **Bass**: Steady 8th-note alternating octaves and root-fifth bounces (`[38, 0, 38, 45, 38, 0, 41, 38]`).
- **Chords**: Polyphonic sustained pads (`[50, 53, 57]` Dm, `[46, 50, 53]` Bb).
- **Lead**: 4-note melodic motifs with vibrato and 16th-note pauses between phrases.
- **Drums**: Steady hi-hats with snare hits, open hat accents (`'O'`), and crash downbeats (`'KC'`).

```
Bar 1-2 (Steps 0-31):   Fmaj7 - Gm7 (Smooth detective stroll)

Bar 7-8 (Steps 96-127): Gm7 - C7 - Fmaj7 - C7 (Turnaround resolution)
```

### Channel Blueprints:

- **Chords**: Polyphonic 7th chord voicings on offbeats (`[53, 57, 60, 64]` Fmaj7, `[55, 58, 62, 65]` Gm7).
- **Lead**: Lyrical, laid-back melody with triplets and syncopation.
- **Drums**: Swing hi-hat groove with light ghost snares and open hats (`['KC', 'H', '0', 'H', 'S', 'H', '0', 'O']`).
