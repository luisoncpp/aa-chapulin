# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players exploring a Spanish-first Ace Attorney-style visual novel in short investigation and courtroom sessions. They need to inspect scenes precisely, follow evidence threads, and understand the current interaction state without losing the comic pacing.

## Product Purpose

El Chapulín Colorado: Ace Attorney combines investigation, dialogue, evidence, and trial logic into a playable retro visual novel. Success means every clue is discoverable, every interaction is readable, and dramatic courtroom pacing remains coherent across Spanish and English.

## Brand Personality

Dramatic, whimsical, retro. The interface should make absurd situations feel legally serious while preserving the timing and visual energy of classic 16-bit courtroom games.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, quiet productivity-tool conventions, glassmorphism, soft pastel minimalism, and interfaces that hide game state behind ambiguous or overlapping controls.

## Design Principles

- Make the investigation state obvious at a glance.
- Let evidence and hotspot geometry match what is visibly painted in the scene.
- Use high-contrast retro drama for important actions while keeping exploration calm enough to read.
- Preserve Spanish/English parity in copy, geometry, and interaction behavior.
- Keep comedy in the writing and timing, not at the expense of player control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Do not communicate hotspot availability through color alone; hover and pointer behavior should remain understandable without relying on animation.
