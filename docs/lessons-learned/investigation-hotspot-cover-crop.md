# Investigation Hotspots Follow the Cover Crop, Not the JPEG

Case 2 plates are 1536×1024. The stage is 960×540. CSS `background-size: cover` + `center` maps the full width and crops ~80px off the top and bottom of the source. Hotspot `x,y,w,h` are percentages of that cropped stage.

Guessing boxes from a written room layout (safe in the middle, clock upper-right, vent upper-left) will miss the painted objects. Overlay the current percentages on a 960×540 cover crop of the actual `assets/bg_*.jpg` before committing. Keep ES and EN copies of the same numbers. Case 3's first plates did this: Cabina B's trophy, coiled booth mic, hallway streaks, and tape shelf were not in the painting, so the plate had to be regenerated instead of sliding empty boxes.

The floor grate in the vault sits under the dialogue strip. The clickable vent is the wall hatch that is still on-screen.
