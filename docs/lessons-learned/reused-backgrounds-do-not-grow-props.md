# Reused Backgrounds Do Not Grow Spec Props

**What to remember:** A later episode that reuses `bg_despacho.webp` or `bg_delegacion.webp` still only has the objects those plates already paint. Case 5’s office-door and ribbon-file hotspots had to land on the vault door and the paper tray, because Examine hides sprites and cannot click a wooden door that was never drawn.

Measure boxes on the 960×540 cover crop of the reused file. Do not keep the spec’s guessed percentages when they sit on empty carpet.
